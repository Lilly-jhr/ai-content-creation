My father learned to repair radios when he was a boy. He watched an older man in the market open them up, and he kept every broken part he could find.
He opened his own small repair stall when he was young. People brought him radios from the whole neighbourhood, and on Sunday evenings he fixed them for free for the old people.
He paid for my brother and me to go to school with that work. Now I build software, and when something breaks I still hear him say: open it up, be patient, find the small part that is wrong.
