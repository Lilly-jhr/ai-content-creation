How it was made

Nothing was edited by hand

One file, storyboard.json, drives eight stages. Every model call goes through one function. It caches by a hash of the exact inputs, retries temporary errors, and writes one line to runs.jsonl. make film rebuilds both versions from a clean clone without any API keys.

1 · script

Translation draft

Gemini 3.8 Flash, falling back to 3.5 Flash-Lite. A native speaker reviews it.

2 · narration

Amharic voice

Addis AI, voice am-sara, chosen blind by family. Slowed 0.9× and warmed by our own code.

3 · subtitles

English captions

Timed to the measured voice. Two lines, 40 characters, inside the safe area.

4 · refs

Character sheets

FLUX.2 klein 4B on Cloudflare. Approved before any shot is drawn.

5 · keyframes

A 16:9 master

1920×1088, conditioned on the approved faces. The vertical version is cropped per shot.

6 · video

Moving shots

Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B on a free Kaggle T4, fp8 weights. If a clip is missing, a Ken Burns move takes its place.

7 · music

Original score

ACE-Step 1.5, four candidates on a laptop GPU. The one that builds was chosen and end-aligned.

8 · assemble

The edit

FFmpeg only. Dissolves, ducking under the voice, two-pass loudness to −14 LUFS.

The run log

Failures are logged as carefully as successes. Most of them were temporary: free-tier “high demand” replies, read timeouts and a dropped connection. Each was retried, or a fallback took over.

Change one line, regenerate one shot

The first audience asked for different wording in s05

The siblings who heard the radio cut wanted s05 to open with “for the ones who would come after them”. After the storyboard was edited, the dry run listed exactly one thing to regenerate. Every image, clip and other line was reused from the cache.

$ make dry-run
  REUSE    narration  s01   818184b14d8fcec2
  REUSE    narration  s02   f61ea6e28898dbe2
  …
  GENERATE narration  s05   64d14f5a3916df0c  changed: text
  REUSE    narration  s06   f5b90a7077870ed0
  …
  RENDER   assemble   (deterministic FFmpeg, no model calls)

The brief, and where each part is answered

Requirements and evidence

The brief asks forWhere it is answered

Made with AI. Every frame, the voice and the music in this film were generated by AI models, then directed and assembled by me. The characters are fictional, painted AI stand-ins for real family members, not photographs. The family consented to the story being told this way.

Models: Addis AI (Amharic voice) · FLUX.2 klein 4B (images) · Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B (motion) · ACE-Step 1.5 (score) · Gemini (translation draft) · FFmpeg (edit).
