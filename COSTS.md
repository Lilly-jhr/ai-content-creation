## Observations
- **Narration latency is the surprise.** Addis AI took a median of about 16 s per line in steady state, but 74–227 s on bad calls.
  Read timeouts and connection resets accounted for 7 of 24 narration attempts (FAILURES F12). That is why narration
  runs as a background job and why caching mattered: a full re-take costs about 15 min, a cached rebuild costs 0 s.
- **Gemini free tier availability, not price, was the constraint.** `gemini-3.8-flash` failed 9 of 11 attempts with 503 "high demand"
  or 180 s read timeouts. `gemini-3.6-flash` failed 6 of 6 (FAILURES F9).
- **Images are cheap and fast:** a median of 13–19 s per FLUX.2 klein call. The 1920×1088 16:9 master costs about 3× the neurons of
  a 1024² frame (12 vs 4 output tiles). The daily free quota (10k neurons) was the planning constraint, and it reset at 03:00 EAT.
- **Video is the expensive stage:** Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B at 544×960, 81 frames, 30 steps took **17.3 GPU-minutes per 3.4 s clip** on a T4,
  plus about 6.5 min of one-off setup per kernel run (weights download and the 152 s CPU text encoding).
  The first attempt cost 6 s before running out of memory (F15).
